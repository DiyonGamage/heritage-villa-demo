### Verified 20 Nov 2026 · Google Maps, live

## Verdict

**Heritage Villa Ahangama is the target.** It is a materially better fit than the dental clinic: a verified mobile number, 4.9 stars, recent reviews, 56 real photos on Google, and no website. Most importantly, the pitch is arithmetic rather than taste — Booking.com takes roughly 15% of every night, so a handful of direct bookings pays for the site.

Two other villas looked good on rating and price but **already have websites** — disqualified.

## The approach

**English first.** Villa guests are foreign and the property markets in English. But the person answering 077 049 8823 is likely local staff, so keep the Sinhala version ready for forwarding.

### Voice note (English, 25–30 sec)

> "Hello, is that Heritage Villa? I'm Diyon. I found your place on Google — 4.9 from 29 reviews, and guests keep saying how calm it is. But I couldn't find your own website — can I just check, is that right? Bookings through Booking cost you around 15% of every night. Seven direct nights a month would pay for a simple site with your own photos and a WhatsApp booking button. I made a small sample. Could you forward it to the owner?"

Why it works: praise from *their own reviews*, one checkable question, one hard number, one easy ask that staff can actually do. Do not say "you don't have a website" as a statement — ask them to confirm it.

### Sinhala version (for the manager)

> "ආයුබෝවන්. මම දියොන්. Heritage Villa ගැන පොඩි දෙයක් කියන්න ඕනේ. Google එකේ 4.9යි, reviews 29යි — හැමෝම කියන්නේ ගොඩක් සදදායක තැනක් කියලා. ඒත් ඔයාලගේම වෙබ් එකක් මට හම්බුණේ නෑ — ඒක හරිද? Booking හරහා එන බුකිං එකකට 15%ක් යනවා නේද? සතියකට සැරයක් කෙලින්ම බුක් වුණොත් වෙබ් එකේ මිල මාසෙකින් ආපහු එනවා. මම පොඩි sample එකක් හැදුවා — මේ ගැන කතා කරන්න කෙනෙක් ඉන්නවද? ස්තූතියි."

Meaning: "Hello, I'm Diyon. One small thing about Heritage Villa. On Google it's 4.9 with 29 reviews — everyone says it's a very calm place. But I couldn't find your own website — is that right? Bookings through Booking cost about 15%, don't they? One direct booking a week would pay for the site in a month. I made a small sample — is there someone I can talk to about this? Thank you."

**Timing:** it is open until 11:30 pm, so this one can be sent in the evening — unlike the dental clinic. Try 5–8 pm.

### If they reply

- **"Is this the owner?"** → you're talking to staff. "Could you forward it to the owner? I'll keep the message short."
- **"We're on Booking/Airbnb"** → "Exactly — that's where the 15% goes. This would sit beside those, not replace them."
- **"Send email"** → "I'll send it too, but this link here is easier to show the owner."
- **"How much?"** → don't quote in text. "Can I show you for five minutes on a call first? Then one price."
- **"Not interested"** → "No problem at all, thank you." Stop for two weeks.

## Caveats

1. **"No website" is from the Google Maps field**, the same source that was wrong about nothing so far in this research. Still ask them to confirm rather than asserting it.
2. **Owner vs manager** — the villa has staff. Your first contact probably isn't the decision-maker. Design the message to be forwarded.
3. **Price anchor unresolved.** You previously said LKR 15k felt right and 25k felt risky; the old prospect file carries 60–120k. The ROI math here comfortably supports 25k — settle the number before you send.
4. **Do not reuse the dental demo.** Different customer, different language, different reason to buy. This one leads with commission saved, not trust earned.
5. **Two names on the same property** — Maps says Heritage Villa Ahangama, the physical sign says Heritage Garden Boutique. Confirm which one they trade as before putting a logo/wordmark on a demo.

## Research method

Google Maps via headless Chrome with automation masking (WebSearch returns Chinese travel content for every Sri Lankan query; Unsplash, DuckDuckGo, Mojeek and Bing all bot-wall). ## Demo built — 20 Nov 2026

`index.html` — single file, ~37 KB, no build step. Price confirmed by the user: **LKR 25,000**.

**All photography is the villa's own.** Harvested from the live Google Maps listing with the masked headless-Chrome probe after fixing the URL collector for Google's newer `grass-proxy` format. `=w1200-h800-p-k-no` unlocks full resolution on `grass-proxy` URLs; `=w1600-h1067-k-no` works on the older `gps-cs-s` URLs. Nine graded WebP images in `assets/`: pool hero, bedroom, living room, arched mirror corner, lattice detail, aerial, dusk entrance, garden path. Regenerate with `.sourcing/make-photos.py`; source JPEGs stay in `.research/raw2/` and are excluded from the repo.

**Design:** full-bleed pool hero, calm cream/olive palette (no blue-led UI, no gradients on panels), self-hosted Instrument Serif + Inter, light/dark toggle with OS preference, sticky mobile booking bar, real review quotes with names, "book direct" CTA. Follows the standard accommodation-site pattern: atmosphere first, proof second, one booking action visible everywhere.

**Working parts:** 5 booking triggers open a native `<dialog>`; submitting builds a prefilled WhatsApp message to `077 049 8823` (tested end to end). No horizontal overflow at 1440/390/320 px in either theme. HTML validated — no duplicate IDs, no broken anchors, no unclosed tags. `.vercelignore` keeps `research.md` and all source folders private after deployment.

**Before sending:** import the repo into Vercel, then replace the three `og:` URLs and the canonical (currently `heritage-villa-demo.vercel.app`) with the real deployment URL and re-push. Confirm the two-name question (Maps vs the "Heritage Garden Boutique" sign) if they reply.
